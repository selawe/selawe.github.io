**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

## Summary

Unblock Cube is a puzzle game that can be played entirely offline. The app does **not** collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no third-party tracking.

## Data stored on your device

The following data is stored **only on your device** (the app's local storage) and is never sent anywhere unless you use the Rankings feature:

- Best scores and stars per level.
- Language choice (Indonesian/English) and sound preference.
- A random installation identifier (token) generated automatically on the device.

Uninstalling the app removes this data.

## Data sent when using the Rankings feature (optional)

The **Rankings** feature is optional. If and only if you open it while online, the app sends the following data over a secure connection (HTTPS) to our leaderboard server:

- **Random installation identifier** (token) — not a personal identity; not linked to any name, email, or account.
- **Gameplay data** per level: move sequence, score, and completion time.

This data is used solely to compute and display the leaderboard. The server re-verifies the moves to prevent fake scores. We do not sell or share this data with third parties for advertising purposes.

## App permissions

The app requests only the **INTERNET** permission, used exclusively to sync the optional Rankings feature. No location, camera, contacts, external storage, or microphone permissions are requested.

## Children

The app is not designed to collect personal data from anyone, including children. The gameplay data sent contains no personally identifiable information.

## Ranking data retention and deletion

Because the identity is random per installation, ranking data cannot be tied to you personally. For ranking-data deletion requests, contact us at [santosahw@gmail.com](mailto:santosahw@gmail.com).
